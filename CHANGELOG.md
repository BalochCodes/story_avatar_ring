## 0.0.1

* Initial release of `story_avatar_ring`.
* Multi-segment animated status/story rings inspired by Instagram and WhatsApp.
* Dynamic segment slicing with configurable gap angles based on total story count.
* Per-segment status tracking: unseen gradient, seen muted tone, and custom highlight styles.
* Live indicator mode featuring continuous breathing pulse / radar wave animation.
* Interactive scale-down bounce feedback on tap and long-press gestures.
* Corner badge pinning support (e.g., "+" add story icon, "LIVE" pill badge).
* Pre-built aesthetic palettes: Instagram Sunset, WhatsApp Teal, Close Friends Green.
